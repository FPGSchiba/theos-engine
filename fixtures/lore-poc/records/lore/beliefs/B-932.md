The cautionary tale every novice learns. Vale, the most gifted fighter of his generation, sought a way to make the river feed upon itself so that each strike fed the next without end. For a season he was unstoppable; then his own power doubled upon itself until it burst the gates from within. The sects forbid the devouring forms as wisdom, not cowardice: the river turned back on itself does not stop.
