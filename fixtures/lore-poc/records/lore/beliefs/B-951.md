The cautionary tale told to every apprentice. Halvarr would not learn on Grandfather Ember like the others. He found Vareth's note, almost; the Quarrel never fully stilled, but he reached for her strength. For one breath he raised a tower of ice that dwarfed anything a mortal could shape; then her cold came back down his own song. They found him in the spring, a man of blue glass, mouth still open on a note he never quite found.
